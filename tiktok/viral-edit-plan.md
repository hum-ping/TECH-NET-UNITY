# Viral TikTok Edit Plan

## Deliverable
- Format: 9:16 vertical
- Resolution: 1080x1920
- Frame rate: 30 fps
- Duration: ~15 seconds
- Goal: maximize first-second retention, replays, shares, and comments

## Edit Timeline
- **0.00–1.00s — Hook:** Open immediately on the most surprising/funniest moment from the reference. Add a tight punch-in, subtle impact/zoom, and whoosh. On-screen text: `WAIT FOR IT… 😂`
- **1.00–4.00s — Setup:** Rapidly establish the situation with only essential footage. Use cuts/reframes every 1–2 seconds; preserve important natural audio.
- **4.00–8.00s — Build:** Increase anticipation with quick cuts and progressive punch-ins. Text at the strongest setup point: `HE REALLY DID THAT 💀`
- **8.00–10.50s — Payoff:** Brief slow motion immediately around the key unexpected action. Add record scratch immediately before it, then a strong bass/impact hit at the payoff. Snap back to normal speed.
- **10.50–11.50s — Reaction:** Keep the clearest reaction/comedic beat; use a subtle camera shake only if it feels natural.
- **11.50–13.20s — Replay:** Repeat the key moment from a slightly reframed crop, with no dead time. Text: `DID YOU SEE THAT? 😂`
- **13.20–15.00s — Loop:** Short replay/lead-in that visually and rhythmically connects back to the opening frame. Small CTA: `SEND THIS TO YOUR FRIEND 😂`

## Visual Treatment
- Preserve the original subject, action, comedic/emotional moment, and concept, but create a fresh edit rather than copying the source edit.
- Crop/reframe for vertical composition; keep the main subject sharp and centered when possible.
- Use subtle punch-in zooms, dynamic reframing, realistic motion blur, and occasional quick camera shake.
- Remove dead time and pauses.
- Natural exposure and contrast; no heavy filters.
- No artificial faces, warped objects, black bars, watermarks, creator branding, or unnecessary overlays.

## Audio
- Use an upbeat TikTok-style instrumental that is currently available for use in the editor/platform, with no copyrighted lyrics unless properly licensed.
- Start with a short whoosh/impact.
- Build the beat toward the payoff.
- Add record scratch immediately before the unexpected moment.
- Add bass/impact hit at the payoff.
- Keep dialogue and important natural sounds above the music.

## Export
- H.264 MP4
- 1080x1920
- 30 fps
- High-quality bitrate suitable for TikTok
- Audio AAC, 48 kHz
- Verify there are no black bars, watermarks, creator branding, or accidental silence before posting.
